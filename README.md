# Songs For Hope 2026

Sponsorship deck for the Seeds of Hope Foundation's annual benefit concert at The Drake Underground, Toronto.

## Live site

Hosted on Vercel. Once the Vercel integration is connected the public URL will go here.

## What's inside

`index.html` — the entire deck. Single-file build: inline CSS, embedded SVG icons, embedded base64 imagery. No external dependencies beyond Google Fonts.

## The event

**Songs For Hope 2026** is the annual benefit concert presented by the **Seeds of Hope Foundation** at **The Drake Underground** in Toronto. Two acts: Sisters & Brothers (opening) and Every Second Tuesday (headlining). All proceeds support Seeds of Hope's programs — 5,000 hot meals served every week, plus housing, mental health, and life-skills support for Toronto's most vulnerable communities.
